# Brand — FlowEarn

_Status: active_

FlowEarn feels energetic, credible, and transparent. It pairs an off-white
editorial canvas with near-black surfaces and a sharp electric-lime accent.

## Palette

- Canvas: `#F5F5EF`
- Surface: `#FFFFFF`
- Ink: `#171913`
- Dark surface: `#13150F`
- Primary accent: `#D9FF57`
- Sky support: `#A9D9FF`
- Sun support: `#FFCC66`
- Muted text: `#6E7267`
- Border: `#DFE1D8`

## Typography

- UI and display: Manrope
- Metrics, wallet addresses, and technical labels: JetBrains Mono

## Voice

Short, direct, and concrete. Explain exactly what unlocks, what is provisional,
and what can be withdrawn. Never imply that every view creates an on-chain
transaction.

## Product language

- Use “campaign,” not “listing.”
- Use “live earning” for provisional accrual.
- Use “available” for finalized, withdrawable USDC.
- Use “private settlement” for the final MagicBlock payout.

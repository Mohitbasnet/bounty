# Product Positioning

## Hard Truth

Paying creators according to views is not a unique product category.

Superteam Earn already aggregates Web3 opportunities. Whop Content Rewards
already supports funded creator campaigns with per-view rates and payout caps.
Shillers already offers funded, pay-per-view X campaigns for token projects with
official metric polling and on-chain settlement. Broader products also cover
creator performance campaigns, affiliate attribution, and automated rewards.

FlowEarn should not position itself as another bounty marketplace or as merely
"Whop on Solana."

## Recommended Category

**Performance payroll for Web3 creators.**

The product promise is:

> Every qualified creator earns under a public formula as verified results grow.

The V1 wedge is:

> Pre-funded performance payroll for original creator-led Web3 content, with
> live earning states, momentum bonus epochs, and private USDC withdrawal.

## Who We Serve First

### Customer

Web3 brands, wallets, protocols, and consumer products that currently run
creator campaigns through spreadsheets, DMs, screenshots, and manual wallet
transfers.

### Creator

Small and mid-sized writers, video creators, visual storytellers, and educators
who often lose winner-take-all bounties to larger accounts or wait weeks for a
sponsor to choose and pay them.

V1 is not for developer work, generic clip farms, broad influencer discovery,
or every type of freelance work.

## The Product Contract

Every FlowEarn campaign must satisfy five rules:

1. **Funded first:** the campaign does not open until its USDC budget is funded.
2. **Rules before reach:** eligibility, reward blocks, cap, validation, and
   deadline are visible before a creator submits.
3. **No winner selection:** after content approval, every creator who reaches
   verified performance earns under the same formula.
4. **Visible money states:** creators can distinguish estimated, provisional,
   finalized, reserved, and paid balances.
5. **Creator-controlled settlement:** once earnings are finalized, the sponsor
   cannot arbitrarily cancel them; the creator can request private USDC
   withdrawal.

These rules, taken together, are the V1 differentiation. If any one of them is
removed, FlowEarn becomes much closer to an ordinary bounty or content-reward
platform.

## Why Creators Come

- They do not complete speculative work for a small set of sponsor-picked
  winners.
- They know the earning formula and cap before publishing.
- They can watch verified performance become money instead of waiting for a
  campaign-end spreadsheet.
- Finalized earnings are enforceable by the platform workflow, not sponsor
  discretion.
- They receive USDC rather than an unfamiliar project token.
- They can withdraw finalized earnings privately.

## Why Companies Come

- They buy verified performance rather than uncertain posts or subjective
  winners.
- Their total budget and each creator's exposure are capped.
- Original educational creators are separated from low-quality clipping and
  engagement farming.
- Funding, metric evidence, earning events, approvals, and payouts share one
  auditable record.
- The platform replaces campaign spreadsheets, screenshot checks, payout DMs,
  and manual wallet batches.
- Unused campaign budget can be reconciled instead of silently overspent.

## What We Do Not Lead With

- **Micropayments:** this describes the engine, but can sound like tiny,
  unattractive pay. Lead with live performance earnings.
- **Private payments:** this is an important settlement benefit, not the whole
  reason a company runs a campaign.
- **Solana:** it makes global USDC settlement practical, but customers buy the
  workflow and result, not the chain.
- **Marketplace size:** FlowEarn will initially have fewer campaigns and
  creators than established platforms.
- **Real-time views:** social providers update periodically. Promise transparent
  earning updates after verified snapshots, not second-by-second truth.

## Defensibility

The current UI and payment formula are not a moat. They can be copied.

The realistic moat develops in this order:

1. **Trust:** a public record that funded campaigns pay correctly and creators
   cannot be rugged after finalized performance.
2. **Performance data:** creator reliability, content quality, fraud signals,
   conversion history, and benchmark reward rates.
3. **Workflow integration:** metrics, company analytics, wallet settlement, and
   campaign operations embedded in customer growth processes.
4. **Network effects:** strong companies attract reliable creators; reliable
   creators improve company outcomes and attract more funded campaigns.

Private settlement and blockchain implementation are supporting capabilities,
not durable moats by themselves.

## V1 Product Focus

V1 should prove one sentence:

> A Web3 company can pre-fund an educational creator campaign, and every
> qualified creator can watch verified performance become withdrawable USDC
> without waiting to be selected as a winner.

Required V1 proof:

- One narrow content format: original educational X content.
- One transparent reward formula.
- One authoritative performance adapter.
- One funded USDC campaign.
- One creator-visible earning ledger.
- One private withdrawal path.
- One complete reconciliation report.

Do not add jobs, grants, generic projects, leaderboards, AI scoring, multiple
chains, or many social networks before this loop works.

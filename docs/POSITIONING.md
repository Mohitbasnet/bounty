# Positioning

## One Line

FlowEarn helps companies pay X creators for verified reach in USDC.

## Wedge

- Company marketing campaigns, not token launches.
- Creator-first X workflow, not a generic bounty board.
- Official impression-based earnings, not winner-take-all prizes.
- Stable USDC terms, not arbitrary project-token exposure.
- A low-fee target and refundable unspent campaign budget.

## Competitive View

Shillers validates that funded pay-per-view X campaigns have demand. FlowEarn
adapts the workflow for ordinary companies and USDC creator payouts. The product
must compete through reliable verification, simpler company onboarding, lower
fees, and better creator trust rather than copied branding or source.

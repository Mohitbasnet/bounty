# Version 1

## Scope

V1 is an X-only, USDC-only creator campaign platform on Solana devnet.

## Company Flow

1. Connect the company wallet.
2. Enter company name and campaign terms.
3. Sign campaign creation.
4. Sign the devnet USDC funding transfer.
5. Wait for on-chain confirmation before the campaign becomes live.
6. Review creator submissions.
7. Approve or reject with the owner wallet.
8. Let the 30-minute worker sync official X metrics.
9. Let the daily worker pay accrued creator USDC and the platform fee.
10. Close manually or let expiry refund unspent USDC.

## Creator Flow

1. Connect a Solana wallet.
2. Browse funded campaigns.
3. Publish content on X.
4. Link the publishing X account through OAuth.
5. Sign and submit the public post URL.
6. Wait for company approval and exact X author verification.
7. Track persisted views, accrued USDC, and confirmed payouts.

## Completion Boundary

The interactive devnet vertical slice is implemented. Production V1 still
requires durable queued workers, database and signer migration, replay-safe
nonces, rate limiting, audit logging, and monitoring.

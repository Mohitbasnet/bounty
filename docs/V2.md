# Version 2: Expansion and Trust Reduction

## Objective

Turn a validated X campaign product into a reusable performance-payment engine
without weakening ledger safety or payout reliability.

V2 is not a promise to build every item below. Each capability must be promoted
by measured V1 demand.

## Candidate Capabilities

### More Performance Adapters

- YouTube views and watch-time campaigns.
- Instagram and TikTok creator campaigns.
- Referral, signup, download, and sales conversion events.
- Adapter certification and provider-specific evidence rules.

### Trust-Minimized Campaigns

- Program-controlled campaign escrow.
- Signed metrics oracle.
- MagicBlock Ephemeral Rollups for frequent entitlement updates.
- On-chain claimable balances.
- Creator self-claim.
- Transparent campaign solvency proofs.

### Automation

- Automatic finalization for low-risk submissions.
- Configurable automatic payout thresholds.
- Rules-based fraud scoring.
- Dispute windows and evidence workflow.
- Automatic campaign completion and unused-budget refund.
- Treasury alerts, retry policies, and reconciliation reporting.

### Marketplace and Growth

- Public campaign discovery.
- Creator reputation and campaign history.
- Company reputation and verified funding record.
- Team roles and approval policies.
- Campaign templates and reusable creator lists.
- Notifications, analytics, and exports.

### Production Operations

- Company-isolated treasury model.
- KMS/HSM-backed signer policy.
- KYC, sanctions, tax, and jurisdiction controls where required.
- Service-level monitoring and incident response.
- Data retention and privacy controls.

## What Would Justify MagicBlock ER

ER should be added only when V1 demonstrates a real need for at least one of:

- High-frequency entitlement updates that are too costly on the base layer.
- Program-enforced campaign escrow.
- Trust-minimized creator self-claim.
- A signed metrics oracle with on-chain verification.
- Scale that makes the off-chain ledger an unacceptable trust bottleneck.

## Promotion Rule

A V2 capability enters implementation only when it has:

1. A named customer problem observed in V1.
2. A measurable success criterion.
3. An owner and operational plan.
4. A security and compliance review proportional to its risk.
5. No simpler V1-compatible solution that solves the same problem.

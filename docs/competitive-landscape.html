Where FlowEarn can win

Every qualified creator watches verified performance become withdrawable USDC without waiting to be picked as a winner.

The five-part product contract

01 / FUND

Fund first

No campaign opens before USDC is secured.

02 / DEFINE

Rules first

Formula, cap, validation, and deadline are public.

03 / EARN

No winners

Every qualified creator earns under the same formula.

04 / EXPLAIN

Visible states

Estimated, provisional, available, reserved, and paid are distinct.

05 / CONTROL

Creator withdrawal

Finalized earnings are not subject to sponsor discretion.

Moat path

1. Trust

Build a record of funded campaigns, correct calculations, and reliable payout.

2. Performance data

Learn creator quality, fraud patterns, conversion history, and fair campaign rates.

3. Network effects

Reliable companies attract reliable creators, improving outcomes for both sides.

Primary landmine

X distribution is useful, but an X-only payment oracle is platform risk. FlowEarn needs a first-party click or conversion adapter even if views remain the first demo metric.
